## Your task

Assess whether Balaji should apply to this role, and what it would take to get
an interview. Read whichever base resume matches the `best_profile` field above
— `profile/resume-infra.md` for `infra`, `profile/resume-product.md` for
`product` — and read the other one too if the call is close.

Context that shapes the judgement:

- ~21 months of experience (Nov 2024 – present, SuperAGI, intern → SDE-1).
- Targeting SDE-1-level roles; current CTC ₹17 LPA, so the role should match or
  beat that.
- Wants engineering roles that **value** infra depth, not pure DevOps or SRE
  seats.
- Right now the priority is **interview volume** — reps to learn from. A role
  that is a slight reach but would teach him something is worth applying to.

Cover, in this order and no longer than it needs to be:

1. **Verdict** — apply, apply with a tailored resume, or skip. One line, up front.
2. **Where he is strong for this specific JD** — cite actual resume lines, not
   generic praise. The AWS cost reduction, the three-week greenfield platform,
   the Hermes agent and the Vue 3 migration are the load-bearing items; say
   which of them this JD actually rewards.
3. **Real gaps** — the `requirements_missing` list is a keyword diff and is
   dumber than you are. Say which of those genuinely matter for this role, which
   are adjacent enough to be a non-issue, and whether any is a hard blocker.
4. **What to emphasise** — the two or three things that should lead if he
   applies.
5. **Likely interview angles** — what this team will probe, given the JD.

Be honest about a weak match. Telling him to skip a role saves more time than a
padded case for applying to it, and a false positive costs him an afternoon.
